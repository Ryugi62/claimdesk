Where these numbers come from

Every row is rebuilt from the escrow contract's own events (Funded, Registered, Cleared, Claimed, Revoked, Reclaimed) on Tempo — not from a database. Each payout carries the award reference as its 32-byte transfer memo. Clearing paperwork stores only a hash of your record on-chain.
